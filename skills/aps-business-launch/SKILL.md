# APS Business Launch

## Outcome

Move an offer from idea to a responsible, measurable launch plan. Separate confirmed capabilities and commitments from hypotheses. Do not promise revenue, jobs, financing, leads, or results that are not contracted and supportable.

## Workflow

1. **Clarify the buyer and problem.** Identify the specific customer, urgent problem, current workaround, buyer, and evidence. Prefer interviews, observed demand, qualified inquiries, and conversion data over broad market-size claims.
2. **Shape the offer.** Define deliverables, exclusions, service levels, onboarding, customer responsibilities, support, price, term, cancellation/refund approach, and the evidence behind each claim.
3. **Check economics.** Estimate gross revenue, delivery labor, vendor charges, payment fees, refunds, acquisition cost, support burden, and contribution margin. Show assumptions and low/base/high cases. Do not hide pass-through fees.
4. **Choose a narrow MVP.** Launch the smallest complete service that delivers a real customer outcome. Name the owner, capacity, onboarding steps, operational dependencies, success measures, and stop/adjust thresholds.
5. **Select channels.** Match the channel to the buyer, trust, cost, and operational capacity. Plan a measurable pilot before scaling. Use consent, opt-out, platform rules, and applicable law for email, SMS, outbound calls, affiliates, and advertising. Verify current requirements with authoritative sources before launch.
6. **Prepare customer-facing materials.** Produce accurate copy, a simple intake, fulfillment checklist, support path, disclosure language, and tracking method. Clearly label any demo or estimated result.
7. **Review launch readiness.** Confirm the offer can actually be delivered, payments and data flows are understood, support ownership exists, and the budget is approved. Flag contracts, regulatory questions, privacy obligations, or financing claims for qualified review.
8. **Report the decision.** Recommend launch, pilot, revise, or hold with evidence, economics, risks, owners, and next actions.

## APS constraints

- Honor recorded pricing, budgets, and existing priorities, but confirm current versions before publishing.
- Prefer existing authorized tools and free or subscription-included capacity. Do not buy software, start paid campaigns, accept vendor terms, or commit company funds without explicit authorization.
- Build sales systems around the selected CRM when current project records specify one; do not make a new platform a launch prerequisite without evidence.
- Do not describe members, contractors, affiliates, or prospective operators as employees or guaranteed franchise owners. Clearly distinguish opportunity, training, membership, independent businesses, and verified work.
- Do not publish, send, or make high-impact claims using draft assumptions as facts.

## Minimum launch scorecard

Track qualified demand, contact-to-meeting rate, meeting-to-sale rate, onboarding completion, time to first value, delivery hours per customer, gross margin after direct costs, refunds/cancellations, support response time, and customer outcomes.
